Honours Project

Dynamic Partial Reconfiguration
of YOLO Models in FPGA

Aug 2025 – Present

FPGAYOLODPREdge ComputingXilinx Vivado

Problem Statement

Deploying full YOLO models on resource-constrained FPGA drones leads to excessive power consumption and thermal throttling during extended aerial surveillance missions.

Approach

1

Hierarchical Data Redundancy Elimination

Developing a hierarchical model to identify and eliminate redundant data prior to processing by analyzing frame-to-frame similarity.

2

Dynamic Partial Reconfiguration

Implementing Xilinx DPR to dynamically swap YOLO layers at runtime, activating only layers needed for current scene complexity — reducing idle power by 40–60%.

3

Optimized Inference Pipeline

Hardware-accelerated preprocessing, quantized inference, and efficient post-processing for real-time edge computing.

Expected Impact

Power Reduction40–60%
Throughput2–3x
Flight Time Increase~30%
